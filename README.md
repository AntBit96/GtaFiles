## What's inside `vice_economy_data.txt`

The file reads like a real game data table, with a file header, `[SECTION]` blocks, item IDs,
aligned columns, localization keys and a changelog.

- **Currency definitions:** the primary in-world currency plus a "Legacy Cash" fallback.
- **Exchange rules and ATM network:** in-world cash conversion and ATM locations by district.
- **Vendors:** weapons, apparel, food and car tuning, with prices and unlock conditions.
- **Vehicles:** classes, prices, top speeds, seats and dealers, from compacts to private jets.
- **Properties:** safehouses and businesses with purchase prices, daily income and raid rules.
- **Mission rewards:** story chapters, side jobs and heists, with payouts and respect gain.
- **Activities:** poker, slots, darts, fishing, drag strip, jet-ski circuit and treasure dives.
- **Wanted-level penalties:** fines and hospital bills for one to five stars.
- **Phone apps:** an in-world wallet app and a fictional stock-ticker app.
- **Radio ads, billboards and achievements**.
- **English localization strings** for every key referenced in the file.
- **Debug toggles and a changelog**, in a typical game-file format.

## Districts covered

Ocean Drive, Little Havana, Downtown, Port Gellhorn, the Keys, Grassrivers, Airport, Marina,
